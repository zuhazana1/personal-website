A closer look

FitHub lets people buy and sell clothing with each other. Our team of five built it with Python, Flask, Streamlit, and MySQL. I designed the database schema and the API layer, which let users search listings by size, category, and style preferences at the same time.

Kalshi Paper. As a business consultant for Kalshi, I put together a paper that used a cost-benefit analysis to turn an open question into a clear recommendation.

Manu Real Estate Report. During my business advisory internship at Manu Real Estate, I wrote a report analyzing the firm's operations, with strategic planning across five areas and a focus on improving profitability.

Stock Data Pipeline. For DS 3500, I built a pipeline that pulls stock and economic data from Alpha Vantage and FRED. Each source formatted things differently, so a lot of the work was lining the data up and handling gaps without the pipeline breaking.
