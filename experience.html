Experience and Interests

Most of my experience falls into three areas: finance, tech, and running things day to day. They overlap more than you'd expect, and the timeline below shows how.

On the finance side, I've done business advisory work at Manu Real Estate, consulting work with Kalshi, and quant research for a student investment group, where I built strategies using market data. Through Beta Alpha Psi and Case Consulting Group I've worked on consulting projects for outside companies. This spring I'm starting a Tax Analyst co-op at Focus Partners in Boston.

On the tech side, I took computer science courses through Khoury College and spent last summer as a product QA engineer intern for an online education platform, testing features before they reached students. Now I'm taking data science courses, including a data visualization class where my group is working on a project about Boston transportation.

The third area is less formal but just as useful. I've worked in restaurants for years, first as an assistant manager at a boba shop, where I handled rushes and trained new staff, and now at a ramen restaurant. Before college I was Senior Patrol Leader in Scouts and competed nationally in FBLA and DECA.

When I'm not doing any of that, I'm usually climbing. I started out bouldering on my own and joined NU Rec Climbing this past year for outdoor trips.
